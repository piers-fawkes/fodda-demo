4. **B2B Procurement Moves From Phone Orders to Marketplace Workflows** (Score: 0.816)
   - **Associated Brands**: MaxAB,Moglix
   - Fragmented B2B procurement that historically relied on relationship-driven phone or in-person ordering is being operationalized through digital marketplaces that provide searchable catalogs, standardized ordering, and managed fulfillment. These platforms centralize demand aggregation and transaction logistics (ordering, payment, delivery coordination), reducing manual coordination between buyers and suppliers.

5. **Premium sporting gear released via limited drops and member access** (Score: 0.815)
   - **Associated Brands**: Paddletek,Alpinestars
   - Sporting-goods brands are distributing select high-end hard goods through controlled scarcity—limited-quantity drops and athlete-linked small runs—and sometimes restricting access via membership/loyalty perks, rather than offering these premium items as continuously available, broadly distributed SKUs.
